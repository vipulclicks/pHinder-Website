What is pHinder?

pHinder is an iOS app designed for students to learn about pH, explore how the scale works, test substances with different indicators, and compare real-world examples, all through simple, hands-on experiments.

How it works.

1

Explore the pH Scale

See how pH works on a scale of powers of 10, from acidic to basic.

2

Pick an Indicator

Choose an indicator like litmus or phenolphthalein and see how it detects acids and bases.

3

Run the Experiment

Test a substance and watch the indicator change color to reveal its pH.

4

Compare & Learn

Compare real-world examples like lemon juice and baking soda to build true intuition.

From the classroom to the kitchen, pHinder turns pH into something you can see, test, and understand.
